# Tracks: large ideas tried beside the main line

Kept by the research agent. A track is an idea too different from what the lab has to be one
option in someone's batch; it is tried in stages, apart from the main recipe, and joins it only
when it has proved itself. The rules are in `PROGRAM.md` ("Tracks") and `roles\research.md`.
Each track has its plan in `tracks\T-00N-<name>.md`.

Headings must keep the form `### T-001 | title | status`, with status one of `proposed`,
`active`, `parked`, `adopted`, `abandoned`. Newest first. At most two are `active` at once.

    ### T-001 | an agent that learns to act in the world | active
    - idea: one line
    - stage: 1 of about 3, "task and scripted baseline"; waiting for: data (environment), eval (task score)
    - GPU minutes: 0 used of 40 allowed for this stage
    - last decision: 2026-11-15 opened
