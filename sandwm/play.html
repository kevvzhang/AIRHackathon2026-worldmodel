What am I looking at?
  • Reality is the falling-sand simulator the training data came from.
  • Imagination is the world model dreaming. At a sync its encoder sees the true frames once; after that the predictor only gets its own previous latent states plus your brush actions, and rolls forward step by step. The picture is the decoder's reading of the imagined latent vector.
  • Reconstruction is decode(encode(reality)): the most a single latent vector can show. Detail missing here cannot appear in the imagination either. Mistakes highlights cells where imagination and reality differ.
  • In the chart, nothing changes is the baseline of a frozen latent (the world as it was at the last sync). The model is doing useful physics when the blue line stays above the orange one.
  • Try: pour sand onto a ramp, fill a cup with water, erase a wall under a pile, or switch off model sees the brush to check that the model really uses your actions.
